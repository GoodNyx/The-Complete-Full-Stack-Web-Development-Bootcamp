A delicious, vibrant breakfast staple perfect for pairing with fresh bread or fried yam.

Ingredients

  • 2 large fresh eggs
  • 1 small plum tomato (chopped)
  • 1/2 small red onion (finely diced)
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper / ata rodo (minced)
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • A pinch of salt and seasoning cube to taste

Instructions

  1. Break the eggs into a clean bowl and whisk thoroughly.
  2. Add the chopped tomatoes, diced onions, minced pepper, salt, and seasoning to the eggs. Whisk again to combine.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
  4. Pour the egg mixture into the pan, swirling it gently to ensure it spreads evenly.
  5. Fry for about 2 minutes until the bottom sets, then carefully flip to fry the other side.
  6. Serve hot with fresh soft bread or tea.
